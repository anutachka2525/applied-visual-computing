OECD Income Distribution Database

Source: OECD Income Distribution Database (Gini coefficient, disposable income, current definition).

The Gini coefficient measures income inequality within a country. It ranges from 0 to 1, where 0 means perfect equality (everyone has the same income) and 1 means maximum inequality (one person receives all income). Values closer to 1 indicate a more unequal income distribution.
